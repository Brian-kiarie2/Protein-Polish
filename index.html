About ProteinPolish

ProteinPolish is a free, login-free tool for cleaning protein structure files (PDB) before molecular docking or simulation. It runs entirely in your browser, so your structures are never uploaded to a server.

What it does: removes waters, selected ligands and alternate conformations, keeps chosen chains and residue ranges, previews before and after in 3D, reports close contacts, numbering gaps, disulfides and a Ramachandran plot, suggests an AutoDock Vina box, and exports PDB, mmCIF and a Vina config.

What it does not do: add hydrogens or missing atoms, assign charges or minimize energy. For those, use the generated PDBFixer script. Always check results before publishing.
